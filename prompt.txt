Write one joke about cats: a single line, under 200 characters, nothing else.

The joke needs a setup and a turn that lands on the first read — something a
person would actually retell to a friend the same day. Aim for a surprise, a
misdirection, or a fresh angle on cats, not a pun everyone has heard before
(no purr-fect, no meow jokes, no "cats rule the house" cliché). Keep it short,
no padding, no wind-up, and no explanation after the punchline.

My angle: the cat knocking things off the table is not being rude — reframe
it as a job, quality control, and let the verdict land on everything the
owner owns failing inspection. The humour comes from treating a familiar
annoying cat habit as serious, competent work.
